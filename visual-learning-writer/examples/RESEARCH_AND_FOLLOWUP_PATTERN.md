# Style exemplars from the user's supplied material

These are *patterns for explanation*, not facts to reuse in an unrelated lesson.

## A research finding, plain language

From the provided `What_The_Research_Says(2).md`: the explanation begins with what researchers **actually did**, then their measurable result, then a plain-language interpretation and finally a decision for the project.

- **Study:** Researchers compared predictions of difficulty from question text against known performance on 667 real exam items.
- **Result:** State the two error figures (0.299 vs 0.311) and interpret the limited gain carefully; don't say the model solved difficulty prediction.
- **Meaning:** A question's difficulty also depends on *who answers it*, not only the words in the question.
- **Project implication:** Estimated difficulty before exam data remains provisional.

Why this works: evidence is converted into a real consequence without hiding the uncertainty or dressing the finding in jargon.

## A deeper, targeted follow-up

From `Part8_Answered(1).md`: the writer answers a specific question, "How many SMEs?", by revealing the hidden distinction: *reviewing correctness* and *estimating difficulty* are different jobs.

- First surface the mistaken framing: one SME count is not enough to describe both jobs.
- Show the two jobs side by side (one detects defects, one averages noisy judgments).
- Add a small numerical table or calculation only when it resolves a real decision.
- State assumptions (e.g., independent reviewers) and the real-world limit.
- End with a concrete operational recommendation for the project.

Why this works: the extra detail directly resolves the user's question. It is **not** a request to rewrite the whole system design file. In particular, `Design_State_And_Examples` is project context, not the mandated prose style.

## Transfer rule

When asked "explain section 8", locate section 8, its immediate context and the user's confusion. Answer *that* first. Do not default to re-summarizing the entire research file. When converting a full research report, keep each finding's evidence distinct from what it implies for the project.
